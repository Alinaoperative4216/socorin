## 🛠️ How to Use socorin (Quick Guide)

### Taking a Screenshot
1. Open socorin.
2. Click the "Capture" button or press `Ctrl + Shift + S` (Windows).
3. Drag your mouse to select the area you want to capture.
4. Release the mouse – your screenshot appears in the editor.

### Adding Annotations
1. In the editor, look for the toolbar on the side.
2. Choose a tool like "Arrow" or "Text."
3. Click and drag on your image to add the annotation.
4. Adjust color and size using the options at the top.

### Recording Your Screen
1. Click the "Record" button in the main menu.
2. Select the area of the screen to record.
3. Click the red "Record" button to start.
4. When done, click the stop button in the floating bar.
5. Save your video as an MP4 file.

## 💡 Tips and Tricks

- **Use keyboard shortcuts:** Memorize `Ctrl + Shift + S` for quick captures and `Ctrl + Shift + R` to start recording.
- **Annotate before saving:** Always add your notes before saving the file. It's easier to edit everything at once.
- **Record specific windows:** When recording, choose a specific window instead of the whole screen for a cleaner video.
- **Check your audio settings:** If you want to include your voice in recordings, make sure your microphone is enabled in the app settings.
